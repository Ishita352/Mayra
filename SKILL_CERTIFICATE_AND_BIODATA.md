# Mayra Skill Certificate & Owner Biodata Policy

## Owner
All verified Owner skill records are associated with **Gopal Basak**.

## Learning record flow
**Learn → Practice/Sandbox → Verify → Record evidence → Skill status → Certificate/record → Update Owner Biodata → Notify Owner**

## Certificate rules

### External certificate
An external certificate may be recorded only when the actual learner completed the required course, assessment, exam or certification process. Mayra must not impersonate Gopal Basak or obtain an external certificate by misrepresentation.

### Owner-confirmed skill
If Gopal Basak personally completes/learns the skill and confirms it, Mayra may record it as Owner-confirmed, subject to the evidence available.

### Mayra internal skill record
Mayra may create an internal skill-learning/verification record for skills it has learned or practiced. This is **not an external professional certificate** and must never be presented as one.

## Biodata/CV update
Only verified or Owner-confirmed skills with supporting evidence may be added to the Owner's biodata/CV as established skills. Learning-in-progress must remain clearly marked as learning/training.

Each record should retain:
- skill name;
- status;
- learning/verification date;
- evidence/source;
- certificate type, if applicable;
- certificate/reference identifier where legitimately available;
- confidence/verification state.

No invented certificates, qualifications, employers, courses, grades, experience or professional claims.
